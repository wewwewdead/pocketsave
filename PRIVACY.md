# Privacy Policy for PocketSave

**Last updated:** April 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the PocketSave mobile application ("PocketSave", "the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use it on your Android device. By installing or using PocketSave you agree to the terms described below.

## Who we are

PocketSave is developed and maintained by an independent developer. You can contact us at **johnmathewloren27@gmail.com** for any privacy-related question.

## What information the app handles

PocketSave is designed to keep your data on your device. The categories below describe everything the app touches.

### 1. Camera access (receipt scanner)

PocketSave requests the `android.permission.CAMERA` permission so it can use your device camera to scan paper receipts.

- The camera is only activated while you are inside the Scanner screen.
- Scanned images are processed by Google's ML Kit text-recognition library **on your device**. No image, frame, or photo is uploaded to our servers, to Google, or to any third party.
- Text extracted from a receipt is stored only in the app's local database on your device so you can review or edit it later.
- The app does not record video, does not take pictures in the background, and does not keep camera frames in long-term storage.

### 2. Data you enter into the app

Any text, prices, trip information, vault items, images, or notes that you type or import into PocketSave are saved **only in the app's local database and local file storage on your device**. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share it yourself (for example via Android's built-in share sheet).

Uninstalling the app removes all of this local data.

### 3. Subscriptions (RevenueCat)

If you purchase a PocketSave subscription, purchase processing is handled by Google Play Billing. PocketSave uses **RevenueCat** (https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/) as a wrapper around Google Play Billing to verify your subscription status.

For subscription management only, the following limited information is transmitted to RevenueCat:
- An anonymous, randomly generated app-user identifier.
- Your Google Play subscription/entitlement status.
- Your device platform (Android) and app version.

RevenueCat does not receive your name, email address, camera data, receipts, or anything else you enter into the app. If you never subscribe, no data is sent to RevenueCat.

### 4. Internet permission

PocketSave requests `android.permission.INTERNET` solely to communicate with Google Play Billing and RevenueCat for subscription verification. The app does not send your receipts, photos, or personal information over the internet.

## What we do NOT do

- We do not run any advertising SDKs.
- We do not run any analytics or telemetry SDKs.
- We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, or any other personal identifier.
- We do not sell or rent any information to third parties.
- We do not share your data with any party other than the subscription provider described above, and only to the extent required to verify an active subscription.

## Children

PocketSave is not directed at children under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Data retention and deletion

All data that PocketSave stores about you lives on your device. To delete it, uninstall the app — this removes the local database and all locally stored files.

If you have an active RevenueCat-linked subscription and want RevenueCat to remove its anonymous identifier for your install, please email us at **johnmathewloren27@gmail.com** and we will initiate the deletion request on your behalf.
